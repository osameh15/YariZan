# Article cover provenance

Built-in image_gen was used for the new place-value cover. The patterns cover was also newly generated with built-in image_gen; every new article receives its own new image. Both final files are 1536 x 1024 WebP under apps/web/public/blog-covers/.

Prompt for place-value-with-household-objects.webp: Original wide editorial illustration for a Persian family education blog about ones and tens. Match the approved warm detailed hand-painted Iranian home aesthetic, textured paper, teal, ochre and terracotta, natural expressive faces, Persian ceramics and textiles. A mother and elementary-age son arrange craft sticks into two tied bundles and three loose sticks. No words, numbers, logos or watermarks. Landscape 1536 x 1024, central composition for cropping.

Prompt for shape-and-colour-pattern-games.webp: Original editorial cover for a Persian educational blog about repeating shape and colour patterns. Warm hand-painted Iranian home, textured ivory paper, teal, ochre and terracotta, ceramics and patterned cushions. Father and daughter arranging alternating large teal triangles and ochre circles on a wooden table, extra pieces in a tray. Natural expressions, collaborative mood, wide three-quarter composition, no text or logos, landscape 1536 x 1024.
